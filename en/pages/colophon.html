Colophon

Who made this, where to write when a step is wrong, and how much of it has been checked on a Tonverk.

Made by

Sixteen Tracks is written by one Tonverk owner, published by {{ site.privacy.controller }} The prose is drafted with an AI, Claude, from the manual for OS {{ OS }} and from what the community has found out; a machine cannot press a key, so no step is called checked until a person has done it on a unit. The mark at the foot of every step says which of the two you are reading. If a step does not do what it says on your unit, or a key is named wrong, write to {{ site.privacy.contact }} with the session and step number. Corrections are listed below as they come in.

The mark at the foot of every step

  • 10.3 The step is written from that section of the Tonverk user manual for OS {{ OS }}.
  • ↗ community The step rests on something the manual does not say: a forum thread, a video, a developer's answer. The link is the source.
  • checked on the unit The step has been done on a Tonverk running OS {{ OS }} and it behaved as written.

Until a step is checked, the manual is the authority and your unit may differ. How far the checking has got:

Where the unit disagreed

Nowhere yet: no session has been through a unit. One thing on the drawn panel is known to be unchecked rather than wrong: the mint legend under ARP, CHORD and SONG says On/Off because the manual describes them that way, and the print on the unit has not been checked.

The pictures

The panel and the screens are drawn from the manual's figures and descriptions, not photographed. They show what a step needs and leave the rest out. What each key colour means, mode by mode, is on Key colours.
