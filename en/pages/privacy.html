What is collected

Audience measurement runs on Matomo, installed on our own server at {{ site.analytics.matomo.url }}. Nothing is sent to Google, to an advertising network, or to any analytics company. For each page you open it records:

  • the address of the page, and the page or search engine you arrived from;
  • an anonymised IP address, from which only an approximate region is derived;
  • the kind of browser, operating system and screen size;
  • the time of the visit, and which links you followed onward;
  • whether you jumped to a step, opened a checkpoint, or used a simulator. These events carry only a fixed name and a step number.

The purpose is to see which sessions are read and whether the checkpoints and simulators are used. The legal basis is legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR — measuring an audience in aggregate, which cannot be done without counting.

What is not collected

  • No cookies. Matomo runs here in its cookieless mode. The two things the site keeps on your device are the number of the session you last opened and, if you picked one from the globe, the language you chose, in your browser's own local storage, so the front page can offer a "continue" link and every page can open in your language; neither ever leaves your device and nothing reads them but these pages.
  • No cross-site tracking, no advertising identifiers, no profiling, no data sold or shared.
  • Nothing you do in a simulator — which steps you set, which conditions, what you tried. A simulator event says only that a simulator was used. The site has no accounts and no forms, so there is nothing to type and nothing to store.

When nothing is collected at all

In these cases the measurement script does not load at all:

  • if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control;
  • on any copy of the site that is not at this address: a saved page, an offline copy.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what data relates to you, ask for it to be corrected or erased, object to its processing, or ask for a copy. Write to {{ site.privacy.contact }}. Be aware that because the data is collected without cookies and with an anonymised IP, there is usually no way to connect any stored record to a particular person, so a request may be impossible to fulfil for lack of anything to match you against.

If you are in Italy or elsewhere in the EU and you think this site handles data improperly, you may complain to your national supervisory authority — in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Why there is no cookie banner

Nothing on this site identifies you or follows you: the measurement sets no cookie, and the only items kept in your browser are preferences of your own, the session you last opened and the language you picked, that no server ever sees. There is nothing to consent to, so nothing asks.

What is collected

Nothing. This site is published with audience measurement switched off. No analytics script is included on any page, no cookie is set, and the site makes no request to any server other than the one that serves its pages. There is no form, no account and nothing to type.

What is stored on your device

If your browser allows it, the site remembers which session you last opened, and the language you picked from the globe if you picked one, in your browser's own local storage, so the hub can offer a "continue" link and every page can open in your language. It never leaves your device, it is not read by anyone, and the site works identically without it.

If this changes

If the site ever starts counting its readers, this page will say so first, and what it says will be the whole of it.
