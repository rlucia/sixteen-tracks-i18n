Sixteen tracks,
one piece,
sixteen minutes at a time.

I'm a music lover, not a music professional, and I kept giving this nifty machine too little of my time. I wanted a course that starts from an empty project and ends with a song I could play back; there wasn't one, so this is it, for learning to drive the Tonverk or for learning it again after a gap. Each session puts one thing into the piece: you hear it, you save, you stop. The shelf below holds what the steps teach, for the day the memory has faded and you don't want to replay the lesson. The steps are drafted with an AI from the manual and checked on the unit one by one; the colophon says how far that has got.
